Within the eyes lies the story of the soul

Cora is a deeply sensitive girl who feels everything intensely. Through her eyes—the windows to her soul—you are invited into a museum of memories. Each painting represents a moment that shaped her, preserving the emotions and experiences that made her who she is today. Together, these memories tell the story of her soul and the person she has become.
