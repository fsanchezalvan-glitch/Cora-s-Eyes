Within the eyes lies the story of the soul

Cora is a deeply sensitive girl who experiences every emotion with great intensity. She finds meaning in the smallest moments and carries every memory with her, whether joyful or painful. Rather than letting those memories fade, she preserves them as pieces of who she is. Behind her quiet nature lies a rich inner world, where every experience has shaped the person she continues to become..
